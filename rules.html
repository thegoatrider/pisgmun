Rules of Conduct & Etiquette

Rule Guideline
Respect Everyone Always be respectful to other delegates, the Chair, and organizers during debate and resolution writing. Speak politely and calmly. Never raise your voice.
Address in Third Person Say "The delegate of Brazil believes..." instead of "I think...".
Avoid Interruptions Avoid interrupting another delegate. Always raise your country placard to speak and wait to be recognized.
Be On Time Punctuality matters. Sessions start sharp, just like in the real UN.
Stay in Role Always represent your assigned country's views, not personal opinions.
Follow Dress Code Wear formal attire or school uniform neatly as per guidelines.

🎒 Essential Items to Carry for PMUN:
  • ID Cards: Optional during committee sessions, but mandatory during breaks or movement around school.
  • Research Documents: Carry your research binder, position paper, and opening speech.
  • Electronic Devices: You may carry a mobile, tablet, or laptop for real-time research during committee sessions.
